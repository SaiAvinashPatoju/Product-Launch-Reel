# Exvisit Studio launch film - designer brief

Updated 11 November 2026. This production direction takes precedence over the earlier storyboard exports wherever they differ.

## The assignment

Create a human-designed, human-edited 60-second product launch film for a full-screen 9:16 Meta Reel. The audience is developers, people building with AI, and technical founders. The central message is:

**Understand your project. Direct your agents.**

The call to action is **Download Exvisit Studio**, with `exvisit.in/download` as the proposed destination.

The founder can create motion designs and is deliberately commissioning a human designer for craft, taste, music, sound, visuals and frame composition. Deliver an authored film that reflects that decision.

## Human craft is a requirement

- Design the final frames, typography, visual hierarchy and compositions yourself.
- Animate and edit the film yourself using your chosen professional tools. Standard keyframing, expressions, procedural animation, compositing and rendering tools are welcome.
- Select and edit commercially licensed music, or compose original music. Design the sound with recorded or licensed sound effects and deliberate mixing.
- Use a human voice recording for the final narration.
- Do not use generative AI to create or substitute for final frames, illustrations, video, animation, voice, music or sound effects. Do not use an automated prompt-to-video pipeline to produce this film.
- Declare the origin of third-party creative assets and provide paid-ad usage rights. A licence for personal or organic social use is insufficient when it does not cover paid advertising.

Exvisit is an AI product. Genuine agent output, diagrams and artifacts visible in an authentic product recording are expected demonstration content. That is distinct from generating the advertisement's creative assets with AI.

## What the supplied material means

The storyboard, script and layout sketches were prepared with AI assistance to communicate product facts and proposed timing. They are planning material, not finished creative, a template to trace, or the quality benchmark.

Use the four references to understand attention control, UI staging, contrast and visual progression. Do not reuse their footage, soundtracks, branding or proprietary layouts in the final ad.

The designer should challenge weak framing, awkward timing or a better visual treatment. Bring those decisions to the founder with a proposed improvement. Major changes to the message, advertised capabilities, pricing, CTA or duration require founder agreement.

## Story and look

The proposed structure is a question about understanding AI-written code, an early Exvisit reveal, visible proof across Stream, Editor, Space and Chat Studio, and a clear download close.

- Open with the Exvisit signature forest and mint identity, energetic music and concise storytelling.
- Move to a light background for the UI showcase. Preserve the real app's appearance inside the composition.
- Use real UI interactions and purposeful close-ups. Make the action legible on a phone.
- Treat the soundtrack as part of the story. Use contrast, tension, release and selected accents rather than a constant bed with arbitrary whooshes.
- Let the product and the engineer's understanding carry the future-of-development argument. The draft excludes unsupported claims about replacing teams or all code being AI-written.
- End on a stable download card. The pricing line is **Zero platform fee with your own key**, with **Model usage charged separately** alongside it.

The draft has twelve shots and a 130-word voiceover across exactly 60 seconds. These are proposed allocations, not a recorded timing oracle. Record the read, then refine the edit within the one-minute requirement.

## Product truth and capture

Use one coherent project across the engineering demonstration. The proposed Space question is "How does sign-in work?" Record the matching sign-in flow, source navigation and Add to context interaction in the real app.

The supplied Space screenshot contains checkout, total and subtotal. It is explicitly a layout reference and must not be presented as a sign-in result. Existing product captures may include demo data or older labels. Confirm the final build before recording.

Do not fabricate controls, dependencies, capabilities, passing checks or instantaneous results. Mark meaningful time compression. Preserve real source-to-result causality. Hide keys and personal data.

## Review process

1. Present a visual direction and a small set of original style frames. These should establish the opening, UI treatment and end card.
2. Present an animatic with a proposed music direction and timing. Use human scratch narration if needed. Label temporary audio and uncleared assets.
3. Present the first complete motion edit with recorded UI, human narration and designed sound.
4. Refine from consolidated founder feedback, then deliver the final master and editable source.

Record decisions in `reviews/DECISIONS.md` and production progress in `PRODUCTION.md`. Do not label a review pass approved until the founder has approved it.

## Deliverables

- Final 60-second, 9:16 film: 1080 x 1920 H.264 MP4, 30 fps, with a clean audio master.
- A captioned master, a clean-picture master, and timed captions in SRT format.
- Separate voice, music and SFX stems, aligned to the start of the film.
- The editable project in the designer's chosen application, with collected assets, linked media, required fonts and any plugin requirements documented.
- A clear asset and licence register, including music and sound-effects rights for paid advertising.
- A phone-size readability check and a review of the intended Meta placement overlays before release.

Final software choice, designer assignment, schedule and budget have not been selected in this repository. No public repository, advertising campaign or ad spend has been created.
