# Music, voice and sound

Human-select or compose the music, record human narration, and design the sound using original or licensed recordings. No generative AI audio in the final film.

Record track identity, creator, licence, paid-ad rights and proof of purchase in ASSET_REGISTER.md. Large audio files and stems stay in agreed shared storage, with stable locations recorded here.

No final music, voice or sound effects have been selected or recorded.
